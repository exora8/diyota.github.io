01 Commercial Beverage Advertising

Fresh Start Coffee

Fresh Start was created as a premium coffee campaign designed for modern professionals who associate coffee with focus, productivity, and daily rituals. The goal was to position coffee not simply as a beverage, but as part of a lifestyle and a meaningful start to the day.

The visual direction focuses on warmth, comfort, and realism. Rich coffee tones, soft highlights, natural textures, and editorial typography were chosen to create a premium yet approachable identity. Every visual element was designed to communicate calm energy, consistency, and craftsmanship.

AI was used for concept exploration, visual generation, and creative experimentation. Human direction guided the campaign strategy, visual hierarchy, typography, layout composition, storytelling structure, and final art direction. AI accelerated production, while all communication decisions remained designer-led.

The final campaign consists of a hero advertisement and a complete storytelling carousel that presents the product through branding, lifestyle positioning, and visual consistency. The result is a campaign designed to feel authentic, premium, and commercially viable.

02 Luxury Fragrance Advertisement

Kahf Silverwood

The goal was to position Kahf Silverwood as a refined modern fragrance inspired by timeless elegance, natural sophistication, and masculine confidence. The advertisement was designed to communicate luxury without relying on excessive visual complexity.

Dark natural stone textures, subtle reflections, premium lighting, and minimalist composition were used to emphasize product quality and exclusivity. The visual language focuses on restraint, balance, and sophistication rather than spectacle.

AI assisted in generating and refining visual concepts, while final creative decisions were made through human-led art direction. Product positioning, layout, typography, visual storytelling, and brand presentation were carefully curated to achieve a premium commercial appearance.

The final advertisement presents the fragrance as a luxury product suitable for modern consumers who value simplicity, confidence, and timeless design. The campaign balances realism and elegance to create a memorable brand impression.

03 Automotive Advertising

Ducati Streetfighter V4 S

This project was created to communicate the aggressive performance, engineering excellence, and premium character of the Ducati Streetfighter V4 S. The goal was to build a campaign that feels powerful, modern, and worthy of a flagship performance motorcycle.

Strong typography, architectural environments, cinematic lighting, and bold composition were used to reinforce the motorcycle's identity. The design emphasizes performance, precision, and visual dominance while maintaining a premium advertising aesthetic.

AI accelerated visual development and concept generation, while campaign direction, visual hierarchy, branding strategy, typography, composition, and advertising decisions remained human-directed. The workflow combines technological efficiency with creative control.

The resulting campaign positions the Ducati Streetfighter V4 S as more than a motorcycle — it becomes a statement of engineering, performance, and attitude. The visual presentation is designed to capture attention while maintaining a premium brand image.
